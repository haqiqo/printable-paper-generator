Good to know

Is Kami Print free?

Yes. Every paper type and the PDF download are free, with no sign-up, no ads and no watermark.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your paper is drawn in your browser and the PDF is created on your device. Settings are stored only in your browser, and an image you add never leaves your device.

How do I print at the exact size?

In the print dialog, choose Actual size or 100 percent, not Fit to page. The millimetres then come out exact.

What can I make?

Graph paper, dot grid, lined, isometric, hex, handwriting, sheet music, Cornell notes, genkou youshi, a two-page rirekisho, monthly calendars with Japanese holidays, and planner refills in Bible and Mini 6 sizes with optional 6-hole guides.
